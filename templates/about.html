Content Automation Pipeline

From search trend to finished script.

Most creators spend hours researching trends and staring at a blank page before writing a script.

Signal automates this entire workflow: it scans real-time search trends, ranks video topics by demand, and with one click, writes your full video script and B-roll shot list.

It turns raw data into a ready-to-film blueprint in seconds.

01

Trend signals

Google Trends surfaces related and rising search queries for your niche — a relative interest score, not raw volume, since that data isn't free anywhere.

02

YouTube demand check

The YouTube Data API checks how many recent videos exist for each related query and their average views — a real-world signal of what's actively being made and watched.

03

Topic generation

Groq, running Llama 3.3 70B, turns each raw keyword and its demand signal into an actual video title with a one-line angle on why it could work right now.

04

One-click script automation

We don't just give you a title. Click "Generate Full Script" on any idea, and our AI instantly writes a complete script. It includes a scroll-stopping hook, the main talking points, a call-to-action outro, and a list of B-roll visuals for your editor.

On the demand score: it's a directional estimate combining relative search interest and current YouTube activity — not exact search volume, since that data isn't publicly available for free. Treat it as a compass, not a guarantee.
